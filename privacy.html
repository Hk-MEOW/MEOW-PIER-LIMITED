Sundrift Keep Privacy Policy
Last updated: October 19, 2026

Scope and contact
This policy describes the Sundrift Keep Android game (com.sundriftkeep.game). For privacy questions, contact flewgainfarcrhe@gmail.com.

Information handled by the game
Sundrift Keep works offline and does not request Internet access. It does not create user accounts or collect or transmit personal information, device identifiers, location, contacts, photos or microphone recordings. There are no advertising, analytics, payment or tracking SDKs in the game.

Local storage
The game stores your current battle, best star ratings and sound/motion preferences in private app storage on your device. This information is used to restore your progress and settings. It is not sent to the developer or shared by the game with third parties. The app does not provide cloud backup or cross-device synchronization.

Retention and deletion
Local information remains until it is replaced during play, you clear the app's storage in Android settings, or you uninstall the game. Clearing storage or uninstalling deletes local progress. There is no account to delete.

Support messages
If you independently email the support address, your email provider transmits the address and content you choose to send. These messages are outside the game's offline storage. Do not include sensitive information. Contact the same address for questions about support correspondence.

Children and permissions
The game does not ask users for personal information and does not include social messaging, user-generated content, purchases or advertising. It requests no Android permissions. Store audience and content-rating declarations are provided separately by the publisher.

Changes
This policy may be updated when the game's data practices change. Check the updated date above.
